Our approach

All of our goals.

The principles guiding development, and the capabilities already built around them.

  • Gives plenty of leeway to load-up realistically.
  • Available in a few different languages.
  • Native first, usable in a browser too.
  • A capable automatic placement engine.
  • Manual workflow kept as snappy as possible.
  • Built for several containers at once, not just one.
  • Extremely fast.
  • Still in development. Your inputs could shape the way we imagine the ideal software.

Automatic and manual placement

Let the engine propose a tight baseline, then adjust it to your needs.

Live 3D load inspection

Solid or transparent rendering, hover dimensions, and corner markers show exactly what is happening inside the container.

Not just english

Multi-language support, so every operator on your team can work in their own language.

  • Français
  • Tiếng Việt
  • English

Snappy and fluent interface

Built on Rust, every interaction is milliseconds-fast, which allows us to compute any kind of hints, or detect conflicts in real time.

Built around real packing problems

It allows practical leeway: short overhangs, stacking on mostly flush surfaces, and bridging over gaps.

Low-click, keyboard support and fast workflow

UX designed for speed of use. Customizable key-binds and contextual shortcuts make packing much faster with regular operators.

Cargo list panel

A clearer way to prepare shipment contents, all in one place.

The cargo list workspace brings files, edits, and load progress into one view, cutting down back-and-forth spreadsheet work.

Live search Import & merge files Safe editing
3D viewport

Place fast, align tight, inspect everything.

Drag and drop boxes manually, snap to near-flush surfaces and container edges, or let the automatic engine build a dense starting point. Visualize the loadout with the layered / transparent view, as well as the box types distribution.
